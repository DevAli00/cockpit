COCKPIT

1. Drag Cockpit onto the Applications folder.
2. Open it from Applications.

The first time, macOS will say it cannot check the app (it is not signed with a paid Apple
developer certificate). To allow it:
   System Settings > Privacy & Security > scroll down > "Open Anyway" next to Cockpit.
You only do this once.

Your data stays on this Mac, in:
   ~/Library/Application Support/Cockpit
A copy of the data file is kept there every day (folder "backups", last 14 days).
To move your data to another computer: Settings & data > Backup > Download backup.
